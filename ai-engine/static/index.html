Four AI Microservices Powering Direct Trade

Replacing arbitrary middleman price gouging with verifiable algorithmic trust.

1. Computer Vision Quality Grading

Analyzes surface defect percentage, color uniformity, and skin blemishes. Automatically awards Grade A/B/C with a confidence score (e.g. 96.2%), preventing subjective quality downgrading by traders.

2. Agmarknet Mandi Fair-Price Guardrails

Incorporates regional Mandi modal benchmarks. Dynamically triggers warnings if a farmer panic-sells (<85% of Mandi) or price-gouges (>125%), keeping trades fair and balanced.

3. Demand Forecasting (Facebook Prophet)

Provides a 30-day forward demand forecast for commodities to prevent gluts and price crashes, alerting FPOs to stagger harvest dispatches.

4. Shared Route Planning (Google OR-Tools)

Solves Capacitated Vehicle Routing (CVRP) by batching nearby farm pickups in Nashik/Pune directly to residential society dropoffs in Mumbai, cutting freight by 42.5%.
